About us

A quarry on the Sirdarya River you can rely on

Gold Sand and Gravy extracts and processes natural building materials directly from the Sirdarya River bed.

Builders, developers, road companies and private homeowners across Uzbekistan trust us.

  • Own quarry — direct prices with no middlemen
  • Honest volume — every truck is checked
  • Contracts and invoices for companies
  • Flexible: delivery or self-pickup
